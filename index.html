Short Summary

SetHomePoint is an offline utility app. We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to any servers. Your saved home location and your current GPS coordinates are processed entirely on your device and are never sent to us or any third party.

PART 1: PRIVACY POLICY

1. What We Collect

SetHomePoint does not require an account and does not collect personal information. The app accesses the following device features to function:

  • Location Services: The app requires access to your device's GPS to determine your current location and calculate the distance and bearing to your saved home.
  • Device Sensors: The app uses your device's magnetometer (compass) to determine your heading.

2. How We Use and Store the Data

Your location data is used strictly to display the compass needle and distance on your screen. When you tap "Set Home," those coordinates are saved locally on your device using Apple's standard `UserDefaults` (AppStorage). We have no access to this data. If you delete the app, your saved home is permanently deleted.

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or share your location data. Because the app functions completely offline, your data is only shared with Apple's iOS operating system (specifically the CoreLocation framework) to render the compass.

4. Analytics and Tracking

SetHomePoint does not contain any third-party analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or tracking software. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

5. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

6. Your Rights

Because we do not store your data on any servers, we cannot provide, modify, or delete your data. You have complete control over your data. You can revoke location permissions at any time in your iPhone's Settings, and you can delete all app data by deleting the app from your device.
